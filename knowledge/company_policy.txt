1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes clear operational, ethical, quality, and workplace
standards for Ansari for Books Company. It ensures consistent excellence across
all book publishing, printing production, and promotional campaign activities
while protecting intellectual property, maintaining workplace safety, and
building trust with authors, clients, and readers.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees, editors, graphic
designers, press operators, marketing specialists, interns, and freelance
contractors working with or on behalf of Ansari for Books Company.

3. DEFINITIONS
- Manuscript: Any original written or illustrated work submitted to the
  company for editing, design, publishing, or printing.
- Intellectual Property (IP): All copyrights, trademarks, designs, layouts,
  and unpublished texts owned by authors, clients, or the company.
- Pre-Press & Print Proof: The final digital or physical sample approved by
  the client or editor prior to mass printing.
- Book Campaign: Any marketing, launch event, social media promotion, or
  advertising initiative executed to promote a title or the company.

4. POLICY STATEMENT

4.1 Publishing & Editorial Standards
- All manuscripts must undergo thorough editorial review for accuracy,
  formatting, and compliance with local publishing laws and copyright rules.
- Plagiarism, unauthorized reproduction, or infringement of third-party
  copyrights is strictly prohibited.
- Written publishing agreements must be signed with authors before editorial
  or production work begins.

4.2 Printing & Quality Control
- No print run may begin without a signed final proof approval from the
  project lead or client.
- Printing staff must inspect color accuracy, paper quality, binding, and
  trimming throughout production to minimize waste and defects.
- Press operators and warehouse staff must follow all machinery safety rules,
  wear required protective equipment, and report equipment faults immediately.

4.3 Book Campaigns & Marketing
- All promotional materials, press releases, and social media posts must
  accurately represent the book, its author, and pricing.
- Campaign claims must be truthful and respectful of cultural, legal, and
  community standards.
- Customer and reader contact lists collected during campaigns must be kept
  confidential and used strictly in accordance with privacy laws.

4.4 Confidentiality & Intellectual Property
- Unpublished manuscripts, book cover designs, pricing, and client contracts
  are strictly confidential.
- Staff may not share, copy, or distribute unpublished files outside
  authorized company systems.

4.5 Workplace Conduct & Attendance
- Employees must maintain professionalism, meet production and campaign
  deadlines, and treat colleagues, authors, and vendors with respect.
- Punctuality is essential, especially during active print runs and scheduled
  book launch events.

5. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
- Employees & Contractors: Follow quality, safety, and confidentiality rules;
  meet project milestones; report errors or risks promptly.
- Department Leads (Editorial, Print, Marketing): Approve proofs and campaign
  assets; enforce safety and quality standards; manage schedules.
- Management: Review policy compliance, resolve disputes, maintain legal
  compliance, and update this policy annually.

6. PROCEDURES AND COMPLIANCE
- Pre-Print Sign-Off: Editorial and Design must submit a final proof checklist
  before transferring files to the Print Department.
- Campaign Approval: Marketing must obtain sign-off from Management and the
  author (where applicable) before launching public ads or events.
- Reporting Violations: Any breach of confidentiality, safety hazard, or
  quality failure must be reported immediately to a supervisor or Management.
- Non-Compliance: Violations of this policy may result in corrective action,
  retraining, suspension, or termination of employment or contract.
